# `sedge` -- `test/assert-sedge.py`, one belt per balancer part

The eleventh suite, **the only one built to Factorio 2.1's rule and the only one that runs on it**. Nine clusters over forty parts on a flat scratch surface, 3,500 ticks, base only: four single-edge shapes measured against a bare express belt in the same save with their **port counts asserted as an exact multiset before any rate is read**, and the FOUR ways an edit can ask a part for a second belt -- a script BUILD, a silent ROTATION that raises no event at all, a MERGE whose teardowns belong to `AddPart` and are queued before the compiler sees the cluster they make, and since 2026-09-07 a CURVED EXIT (`scrv`), a belt laid across an occupied part's free face, which is the one of the four a player could not provoke at all before the engine's own curve was classified. Every one is refused in front of its teardown with the standing network still delivering -- `scrv` at **2.000x one belt at 0.00% spread over a window its own refusal happens inside** -- the audit reads `drift=1 unbuilt=0` and never the reverse, exactly one refusal is issued per distinct edge state, and **zero pieces are handed back and zero items spilled over the whole run**.

**`srot` needed a dead belt behind it and that is the curve rule showing through the estate.** Its rig stands a belt on a part's north face pointing EAST, so that rotating it south at t=600 makes it an edge SILENTLY -- and a belt across a face with an empty rear is an output now, so the rig as written would have been refused from tick zero and the rotation it exists for would never have been reached. A feeder at (-1, 49) makes the east state a side-load, which the rule declines, so the belt is inert exactly as it was written to be. **Nothing else in the estate moved for the rule**: `m2`'s `pass` and `fdbk`, `edge`'s fast-replace rigs and the interactive band all have fed rears already, which is what a belt in a LINE always has. The rig table, the red proof (fifteen assertions, ten `[BBB] error:` lines and 64 items on the floor with the predicate disabled) and the two design deviations are in [`agents/single-edge.md`](../../single-edge.md)'s implementation-status section, where the whole port lives.
