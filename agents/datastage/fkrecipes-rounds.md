# FkRecipes: the rounds this mod took

These were subsections of "Cost, research and belt speed" ([`cost-research-speed.md`](cost-research-speed.md)). The long form of every round is [`../fkrecipes-migration.md`](../fkrecipes-migration.md).

## The settings are declared through FkRecipes since 2026-09-01, and the prototypes followed in round two

**The two startup dropdowns moved onto [FkRecipes](https://github.com/Techrocket9/FkRecipes), the shared data-stage library, in round one; the item, the recipe and the technology followed in round two, and `guest/go/data/item.go`, `recipe.go` and `technology.go` are deleted.** Neither half is a staging decision. Round one's prototype arm was written, run and REFUSED BY THE ENGINE, and it is recorded below as the measurement it was; round two moved on a library that had answered every one of the three refusals by name. `guest/go/tune/plan.go` is the plan and its header is the long form; [`agents/fkrecipes-migration.md`](../fkrecipes-migration.md) is both runs and the graded friction list.

**What migrated is two calls.** `lib.LegacyDropdownSettingNeedingLocale(SettingRecipeCost, RecipeDefault(), RecipeOptions(), "a")` and the same for `SettingTechCost` at `"b"`, and `guest/go/data/settings.go`'s whole part in them is `tune.Plan().Emit()`. **`Legacy` is forced rather than preferred**: Factorio persists a player's startup choices in `mod-settings.dat` keyed by the setting's NAME and has no rename mechanism, so a prefixed `better-belt-balancer-bbb-recipe-cost` would be a NEW setting and every player who had chosen a value would silently get the default. The order strings are this mod's own for the same reason -- a generated order is two letters from declaration order, and `order` is a field in the settings dump.

**EACH HOOK NAMES THE HALF IT RUNS SINCE ROUND TWO.** `Emit` reads the running stage and dispatches at RUN TIME, so a guest that calls it links both planners whatever its plan declares -- which is where round one's 21,047-line unreachable function came from. `guest/go/data/main.go` calls `EmitSettings` from `fk_settings` and `EmitData` from `fk_data`, nothing calls `Emit`, and each of the two raises if it is routed from the wrong stage. `fk_data_final_fixes` does not mention the library.

**What round one measured out, and what answered it.** Three things stopped the prototype half then and the first was fatal on its own; all three are library surface now:

| round one measured | round two has |
|---|---|
| **every prototype name carried the mod prefix**, with no Legacy constructor for one: `bbb-balancer-part` became `better-belt-balancer-bbb-balancer-part` and `entity.go`'s `minable.result` then named an item nobody defined -- `Error in assignID: item with name 'bbb-balancer-part' does not exist. Source: bbb-balancer-part (simple-entity-with-force).`, `--dump-data exited 1` | `LegacyItem`, `LegacyRecipe` and `LegacyTechnology`, which emit a shipped name VERBATIM. The argument is the Legacy settings' with a wider blast radius: a prototype name is held by blueprints, logistic requests, crafting queues and other mods' patches, and no single dump says whether anything is holding one |
| **`ItemSpec`, `RecipeSpec` and `TechSpec` carried no `order`**, so the item's and the recipe's `c[splitter]-y[bbb-balancer]` and the technology's `a-b-bbb` were DROPPED -- which moves this mod's row in every crafting menu and in Factoriopedia | an `Order` field on all three |
| **`ItemSpec` carried no `place_result`**, the one field that makes the item place the entity | `PlaceResult`, PRESENCE PROBED against the entity types. That probe is what fixes the order of the `fk_data` hook: `entity()` first, `EmitData` after it, or the plan is refused by name |

So all three prototypes are declarations in `guest/go/tune/plan.go` and the three files that built them are deleted. **The recipe and the technology had to cross in ONE commit** and the reason is none of the three above: the library emits a recipe's `enabled` as "no technology in THIS PLAN unlocks me", `RecipeSpec` has no `Enabled` field, and `Extra` refuses a key the library emits itself -- so a recipe declared while its technology is still hand-rolled comes out `enabled = true`, craftable from the first minute, and moves the data hash.

**And the two verbs this mod wanted the library for are the ones it now uses.** `IngredientsBy` takes one `IngredientChoice` per allowed value of `bbb-recipe-cost`, each ingredient an `IngredientNamed` ladder built from `tune.RecipePlan` rung for rung; `CostBy` takes one `CostChoice` per value of `bbb-tech-cost` whose sources are `tune.TechLadder`, and it carries the prerequisite with the unit, which is the rule this mod wrote by hand. `tune` keeps the ladders and stops walking them.

**THREE BEHAVIOURS MOVED WITH `CostBy` AND ALL THREE ARE ON THE RECORD**: the fallback's science pack was probed even when the fallback was unreachable (a load that used to succeed and refused, AWKWARD, and CLOSED by FkRecipes c7a806e on 2026-09-07: the bullet under "The technology, where the prerequisite moves with the unit" carries the closure), `max_level` travels with the copied unit (a single-recipe technology becoming multi-level in a pack whose source is, AWKWARD), and a `count_formula` source that used to abort the load now loads (an improvement). Each is measured on the engine and pinned by a host test; "The technology, where the prerequisite moves with the unit" is the long form.

**AND THE UNKNOWN-OPTION ARM OF `RecipePlan` AND `TechLadder` IS A SHAPE GUARD RATHER THAN A LIVE FALLBACK.** [Plan] builds one choice per allowed value, so an unknown stored value reaches the library rather than this package's default. Round two's library answered it with a recipe made of no ingredients and a fallback-priced technology; **since FkRecipes c7a806e the library REFUSES it BY NAME**, `fkrecipes: bbb-recipe-cost holds "not-an-option", which is not one of its values`, and the same for `bbb-tech-cost` -- the emitted name, which for this mod's two Legacy settings is the unprefixed one a player's file carries. What makes that unreachable is the ENGINE: an unknown value in `mod-settings.dat` is silently RESET to the default before the data stage runs (measured on both packages, no log line), and a wrong TYPE is refused loudly. Both comments say so now, the test that asserted the dead arm is deleted, and `TestAnUnofferedStoredValueIsRefusedByName` pins the refusal on both dropdowns behind the engine's guard.

**THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION WAS BOTH GOLDEN HASHES NOT MOVING, AND THEY DID NOT.** `make datastage-check`, Factorio 2.0.77, eleven arms green: `base data_raw_sha256 f4fbcaa603abc93b`, `base mod_settings_sha256 196275f867f7f8b5`, `incumbent data_raw_sha256 e7001bf98d6c6771`, `incumbent mod_settings_sha256 196275f867f7f8b5`, plus all eight variant arms and the speed arm. (Round three MOVED `mod_settings_sha256` on purpose, twice, and `data_raw_sha256` not at all; the current hashes are in "Round three" and in the Verification table.) **The 2.1.16 and 2.1.17 golden rows are NOT RUN**: the installed binary is 2.0.77 and the packaged mod is pinned 2.1, so a golden whose engine does not match the binary is a SKIP by construction and `make test` refuses at its engine gate for the same reason.

**And the plan is pinned a second way, on the host.** `go test ./tune/` compares every field of both emitted prototypes against literals TRANSCRIBED from the pre-migration dump rather than derived from `RecipeOptions()` and `TechOptions()` -- a gate that computed its answer from the same source as the thing under test would agree with a defect in that source. Keys compare order-insensitively (fkdata sorts them out anyway) and `allowed_values` compares IN ORDER, because that order is the order of the rows in the settings menu. The two instruments answer different questions: the hash says nothing moved, the field comparison says WHICH one did.
