Version: 0.3.3
Date: 2026-09-14
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a permanent freeze that could happen with another mod installed that automatically crafts the items in your quickbar. It appeared when you enabled your personal roboport, or put a balancer part in the quickbar or on the cursor, and the game stopped responding with nothing in the log. The compatibility item this mod defines for saves migrating off Belt Balancer now places a compatibility part, which becomes a real balancer part on the next tick, instead of placing the part directly. Nothing about building or using a balancer changes. Thanks to PiggyWhiskey for the report and the save.
  Features:
    - The balancer part recipe setting gains a second setting under it where you write the ingredient list yourself: the amount, then the item's internal name, separated by commas, as in "2 iron-plate, 1 splitter". It applies whenever it does not say default, and while it says default the option picked above is what you get, so all six options work exactly as they did and whichever one you had chosen is kept. The recipe setting's tooltip now lists what each option is made of, written the way the field takes it, so you can start from the one you were on. The word none is accepted as well, and makes a balancer part cost nothing to craft; the recycling recipe the game derives from this one goes with it, so where you have recycling a balancer part can no longer be recycled.
    - The balancer research cost setting gains three settings under it that reprice the research one field at a time: the science packs, written the same way as the recipe above and taking science packs only; how many units of science the research costs; and how many seconds one unit takes in a lab. The pack field applies whenever it does not say default, and each number applies whenever it is not 0, so a field you leave alone is decided by the tier picked above exactly as it was before. Where the research sits in the technology tree does not move either: the three fields never change its prerequisite. That is still the tier you picked, or the next belt tier down from it where your mods leave that one unpriced, and no prerequisite at all in a mod set where neither the tier you picked nor any tier below it can be read. Whichever tier you had chosen before is kept.
    - A belt can now be laid over any balancer part, the way a part can be laid over a belt. Until now the parts a balancer was actually using refused it, because each of those carries a hidden belt piece of the mod's own.
  Changes:
    - The options in both cost settings are named rather than spelled out. The recipe options read Default, Cheap, Fast belts, Express belts, Splitter and Express splitter, and the research options read Logistics (default), Logistics 2 and Logistics 3. What each option is made of, or which technology it is priced like, is in the setting's tooltip instead: the recipe lists in the internal names the new field takes, and the research tiers by the technology's own name. The old labels were long enough that the closed dropdown cut most of them short. Nothing any option does changes, and a choice you have already made is kept.
    - On Factorio 2.1, a belt laid where a part was may hand the part next to it a second belt, which that version does not allow. The balancer is then refused and stops until you take the belt away again.
    - Dragging a belt across a balancer now replaces every part it crosses, as dragging one across a row of splitters does.
  Info:
    - The balancer research now copies the whole research cost of the tier you picked, including its level cap, where earlier versions read only the science packs, the unit count and the time. In a stock game nothing changes: none of Logistics, Logistics 2 or Logistics 3 has a level cap, with or without the expansions. In a mod set that gave one of them several levels, the balancer research gains them too, and only the first level unlocks anything: the levels after it cost science and do nothing.
    - A settings file written by a newer version of Factorio is discarded whole when the game is rolled back to an older version, and every mod setting in it returns to its default, this mod's included: the two cost options, the two written fields and the two research numbers. Nothing on the settings screen says it happened, so if you try a newer Factorio and come back, expect to set the balancer's options again.
    - Changing what a balancer part costs to craft edits the one recipe this mod has rather than adding a second one, so an assembling machine already set to make balancer parts loses whatever is in its input slots that the new list does not use. Those items are destroyed rather than dropped on the ground, with nothing said in the log. What the machine had already finished stays in its output slot, and the chests and belts around it keep everything they were holding. A typo introduced into a list that was working does the same thing: the list is set aside whole and the option picked above applies instead, which is a recipe change you did not ask for, and correcting the typo is a second one. Empty the machine before you change the option or edit the field if it is holding something you would miss.

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-08-25
  Features:
    - A startup setting for what a balancer part costs to craft: the default recipe, a cheaper one, fast or express belts, or a splitter of either tier. If an option names an item your mods do not have, the nearest thing they do have is used instead, so it is safe in an overhaul pack.
    - A startup setting for which research unlocks the balancer: Logistics, Logistics 2 or Logistics 3. The cost is read from that technology, so it follows whatever your mods charge for it, and the balancer moves to sit beside it in the technology tree.
  Changes:
    - The hidden belts inside a balancer now run at the speed of the fastest belt any installed mod adds, instead of a fixed speed a little above express. Modded belts faster than that are no longer throttled on their way through a balancer. Nothing changes in a vanilla or Space Age game, where the fastest belt is still slower than the balancer's own.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-08-24
  Major Features:
    - Factorio 2.1 support. 2.1 closed the collision behaviour this mod's belt interfaces relied on, so the rule is now one belt per balancer part: a 4-in 4-out balancer is eight parts, a west column carrying the inputs and an east column carrying the outputs.
    - A map setting, "Allow multiple belts per balancer part", available on Factorio 2.0 only and off by default. A save that already connects several belts to one part keeps working: the mod turns the setting on for that save at first load and says so in chat.
  Features:
    - Turning the setting off while such balancers stand is refused with a chat message that lists every one of them as a clickable map ping, and the map is revealed around each pinged balancer.
    - A belt laid against a part that already has one is refused with the reason on screen, and the belt is returned to your inventory. Returned pieces say which rule sent them back.
    - On Factorio 2.1, opening a save built with multiple belts per part keeps every balancer part and belt in place, recovers what the machines were holding onto the ground beside them, and posts a per-force chat checklist with a map ping for each balancer to rebuild.
    - Balancers adopted from Belt Balancer, Belt Balancer 2 or Belt Balancer 3 that are already built one belt per part compile and run; the rest get the same rebuild checklist.
  Changes:
    - The mod description now leads with what the mod does for a megabase.
